# 08 — Go-to-market plan (12 months: Dec 2026 – Nov 2027)

Owners: Business Development (2), Sales (6), Marketing (7), Community and Venue
Partnerships Lead (13), with Data and Growth (14) on measurement and Finance (12)
on budget. Months 13–18 are in `10_financial_model.md`.
Labels: **[D]** decided, **[A]** assumption, **[O]** open. Product name shown as
**[name]** until legal clears the recommendation (Sayso, see `07_brand_and_messaging.md`).

## 1. The idea in one paragraph

We grow where people already meet face to face. Conferences pay early and put
hundreds of people in one room; the neighbourhood pilot proves the product works
week after week; venues then become a steady monthly income and a permanent
"zone" that keeps the product visible. Universities, coworking spaces and
community groups follow in the autumn. Dating and social venues wait until we
have safety evidence from the pilot. No ads, no data sales, no contact uploads —
growth comes from two phones per handshake.

## 2. Guardrails (apply to every channel)

- Sell only what is live today. Premium, the verified badge and multi-site
  dashboards are sold only from the month they ship (premium May 2027, badge
  Sep 2027) [A].
- Never promise "safe". Describe tools (see brand doc, section 11).
- No per-person tracking for marketing. All results come from aggregate counters
  (handshakes per zone per day, chats, groups, reveal steps).
- No deploy, production change or public launch without Michael's explicit "go".
- One metro at a time until zones get a region prefix [A, from `00_decisions.md`].

## 3. Channel by channel

### 3.1 Conferences and events (owner: Sales, agent 6; support BizDev, agent 2)
- **Why first:** organisers have budget for "attendee engagement", a fixed date,
  and 300–3,000 people who want to meet each other. Early revenue and fast
  feedback.
- **Offer:** event licence by attendance — $1,500 (≤300), $3,500 (301–1,000),
  $5,500 (1,001–3,000), $7,500 (3,000+) [A]. Founding-event price $1,500 for the
  first three events of any size in exchange for a short written case study
  (counts only).
- **What they get:** a themed event zone with its own time window, printed signs
  and badge stickers, a slide for the opening keynote, a counts-only dashboard,
  and a post-event summary (handshakes, groups, peak hour).
- **Targets:** 1 event in Jan, 1 in Feb, 2 per month Mar–Jun, 1 per month in the
  quiet summer, 3–4 per month in the autumn conference season. 22 paid events by Nov 2027.
- **Conversion assumption:** 20% of organiser conversations become a paid event;
  so roughly 100 conversations over 12 months, about 2 per week [A].
- **Attendee adoption assumption:** 25% of attendees make at least one handshake [A].

### 3.2 Sticker and card drops (owner: Marketing, agent 7)
- **Why:** cheap awareness that points to a zone. Each sticker says "Say the code".
- **What:** 5 cm stickers and business-card-sized "how it works" cards left at
  pilot venues, events and partner noticeboards. Only where the venue agrees —
  no fly-posting.
- **Volume:** 2,000 stickers + 2,000 cards in Feb 2027 (about $350 [A]), then
  1,000 of each per month from May.
- **Measure:** a separate venue zone or table word per drop location so the
  counters show which drops lead to handshakes. No QR tracking parameters tied
  to people.
- **Target:** drops produce at least 10% of monthly handshakes by Aug 2027 [A].

### 3.3 Neighbourhood pilot (owner: Community Lead, agent 13; Data, agent 14)
- **Why:** proves repeat use in ordinary life, not just at one-off events.
- **What:** one café, one climbing gym, one bar, in one walkable neighbourhood,
  for 8 weeks: Mon 1 Mar – Sun 25 Apr 2027 [A]. Full plan in `11_pilot_plan.md`.
- **Gate:** the go/no-go decision in the first week of May 2027 decides whether
  the venue channel scales. Go = all six thresholds met; Iterate = 4–5;
  Stop/pivot = 3 or fewer.
- **Targets:** ≥ 600 handshakes in 8 weeks; 2 of 3 venues convert to paid.

### 3.4 Venue partnerships (owner: Community Lead, agent 13; Sales, agent 6)
- **Why:** steady monthly income and permanent visibility.
- **Offer:** Starter $79/month (one zone, weekly table word, table tents, staff
  dashboard), Standard $149 (up to 3 zones or themed nights, live display screen),
  Plus $299 (up to 3 sites, event nights, priority support) [A]. Blended
  average $120/month across the mix [A].
- **Expansion pattern:** after a Go, recruit venues within 1 km of the pilot
  first (density makes handshakes more likely), then the next neighbourhood.
- **Targets (paying venues at month end):** May 2, Jun 5, Jul 9, Aug 14, Sep 20,
  Oct 27, Nov 34.
- **Churn assumption:** 5% of paying venues per month [A]; the targets are net of churn.
- **Sales motion:** walk-in visit, 10-minute demo on the counter, leave-behind,
  follow-up within 3 days; 30-day free trial after the pilot period.

### 3.5 Universities and coworking (owner: BizDev, agent 2)
- **Why:** dense, social, recurring populations; new students and new members
  want to meet people.
- **Timing:** September 2027 term start. Outreach from June (decisions are slow).
- **Offer:** community tier from $400/month per campus or coworking operator
  (multiple zones, themed welcome-week zones, counts-only dashboard) [A].
- **Age note:** 18+ only. University deals cover students 18+; we don't target
  first-year welcome events where many attendees may be under 18 (Legal, agent 10).
- **Targets:** 2 pilots signed by Sep, 3 paying community/enterprise accounts
  (all types) by Sep, 5 by Nov.

### 3.6 Community organisations (owner: Community Lead, agent 13)
- **Why:** run clubs, board-game nights, volunteer groups and meetups create
  small groups — exactly what our group feature is for.
- **Offer:** free organiser starter pack (poster, 50 stickers, host script) for
  groups under 50 people; paid community tier for larger networks.
- **Targets:** 10 organisers using starter packs by Jul 2027, 30 by Nov;
  1 paying community account from Jun.
- **Measure:** each organiser gets a themed zone with a time window; counters per zone.

### 3.7 Dating and social venues (owner: BizDev, agent 2; Trust & Safety, agent 11 has veto)
- **Why later:** highest demand but highest harm risk. Moving early would frame
  the brand as a dating app and invite harassment before our safety tools are proven.
- **Gate:** at least 3 months of venue data with ≤ 1 serious safety incident
  per 1,000 chats, zero unresolved, plus the verified badge live (Sep 2027).
- **Earliest test:** one singles' night pilot in Mar–Apr 2028 (outside this
  12-month window), only with T&S sign-off.

### 3.8 Owned channels (owner: Marketing, agent 7)
- A plain website with the privacy promise word for word, a venue page, an
  organiser page, and a monthly email newsletter for venues and organisers only
  (opt-in, business contacts). No tracking pixels, no ad cookies.
- Press: local news story on the pilot in Apr 2027; conference trade press in autumn.

## 7. Risks to the plan

- **Conferences don't renew.** Mitigation: post-event summary within 48 hours;
  ask for a rebooking before leaving the venue. If fewer than 40% rebook by Sep,
  shift effort to venues and community tiers.
- **Density too low outside events.** If a venue does under 20 handshakes a
  week after week 4, move its tents to the busiest spot or swap venues.
- **One venue has a safety incident.** T&S playbook (see threat model); pause
  that zone, talk to the venue the same day, report the aggregate outcome.
- **Too much revenue from events (lumpy).** By Nov 2027 venues plus community
  tiers should be at least 30% of monthly revenue (model: about 34%).

## 8. Disagreements

- **Sales vs Community on pricing the pilot.** Sales wanted pilot venues to pay
  from week 1 to test willingness to pay. Community: free pilot buys honest
  feedback and staff effort. **Resolved:** free for 8 weeks, but the pilot
  agreement includes a written yes/no on paying afterwards — that's one of the
  go/no-go metrics.
- **BizDev vs T&S on dating venues.** BizDev wanted a singles' night in summer
  2027 for buzz. T&S vetoed until the gate in 3.7 is met. **Resolved:** T&S wins.
- **Cynic on conferences.** "A conference is one day. You'll measure novelty, not
  retention." **Resolved:** conference numbers never count toward the pilot's
  second-conversation metric; we report them separately.
