# 11 — 8-week neighbourhood pilot plan

Owner: Community and Venue Partnerships Lead (agent 13), with Data and Growth
(14) on measurement, Trust & Safety (11) on incidents, UX (8) on materials and
Privacy and Security (9) on the counters.
Dates: **Mon 1 March – Sun 25 April 2027** [A]; preparation from 18 January;
go/no-go decision by **Fri 7 May 2027**.
Labels: **[D]** decided, **[A]** assumption, **[O]** open. **[name]** = product
name pending clearance (recommended: Sayso).

## 1. What we're trying to learn

Events prove people will try it once. The pilot asks the harder question: in
ordinary life, at places people return to, do they use it, keep talking, form
small groups, choose to share more, and stay comfortable — and will venues pay?

## 2. The three venues

| Venue type | Zone | Why | Ideal profile [A] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Café | 21 | Daytime, laptops, regulars, quiet | 150+ customers/day, communal tables, open 7 am–6 pm |
| Climbing gym | 22 | Strong repeat visits, partner-finding is a real need | 300+ members, social bench area, open until 10 pm |
| Bar | 23 | Evenings, trivia or event nights, more social risk | Weekly themed night, trained staff, open until midnight |

All three within a 10-minute walk of each other, so the zones list each other as
neighbours [D: venue zones list neighbours explicitly]. Each zone is accepted
only during that venue's opening hours [D].

### Recruiting (weeks −6 to −3)
1. Shortlist 5 of each type within a 600 m radius. Score on: footfall,
   regulars, a manager who's excited, evening staff presence (bar), wall or
   table space for tents.
2. Visit at a quiet hour with the counter script from `09_sales_kit.md`. Do a
   live two-phone demo.
3. Second meeting with the decision-maker; walk through the pilot agreement:
   free for 8 weeks; tents out during opening hours; swap the weekly card each
   Monday (we bring it); 15-minute check-in every two weeks; a written yes/no at
   week 8 on continuing at the founding price ($79/month for 12 months).
4. Bar-specific checks (T&S): staff know how to handle a customer who feels
   unsafe; a named manager we can call the same evening; no promotion on
   "singles" nights during the pilot.
5. Sign by **Fri 12 February 2027**. Keep one backup per type in case a venue drops out.

## 4. Staff training script (5 minutes)

Delivered at shift change in week −1 and again for new staff. Read aloud,
then demo.

> **1. What it is (30 s).** "[name] lets two customers who've met here keep talking without swapping numbers. They scan the tent or type the zone and this week's word, then say six numbers to each other. That's it."
>
> **2. Your job (30 s).** "Point at the tent. That's all. You don't need to set anything up, and you never see who uses it."
>
> **3. Demo (90 s).** *[Trainer and one staff member each open the page and connect.]* "See — six numbers each, both typed, we're in a chat. Nothing else was shared."
>
> **4. Common questions (90 s).**
> - "Is it a dating app?" — "No, it's for anyone who meets here: climbing partners, quiz teams, regulars."
> - "Does it track me?" — "No location history, no ads, no selling data. Your name and number stay hidden unless you both choose to share."
> - "It's not working." — "Check it's this week's word, and that we're open. If it still fails, tell [Community Lead] — the card has the number."
>
> **5. If someone's uncomfortable (60 s).** "Treat it like any customer problem first: are they OK right now? Then: they can end a chat in one tap and it disappears for both of them, and they can report it in the app. Call [Community Lead / T&S] on the card the same day. If anyone is in danger, call emergency services — the app is not an emergency service."

Staff should never be asked to help someone find out who another user is. We
can't tell them, and they shouldn't try.

## 6. Feedback loops

- **Daily:** T&S checks reports in pilot zones twice a day (11 am, 9 pm); bar
  nights covered by an on-call phone until 1 am Thu–Sat.
- **Weekly (Monday):** Data posts the aggregate counters; team 30-minute review;
  one change chosen for the week (tent position, word font, staff prompt).
- **Fortnightly:** 15-minute venue check-in: what staff heard, any problems,
  counts report.
- **In the product [O, small build needed]:** after someone ends a chat or it
  expires, an optional one-tap "How was it? Good / OK / Not good". It only adds 1
  to a daily total for that answer — not stored with the chat, the person or the zone.
- **Michael:** short weekly email with the numbers and one sentence on what we changed.

## 7. Capturing success stories without identities

1. **Paper postcards** at each venue: "Did [name] help you keep talking with
   someone? Tell us in a sentence. Please don't write names." A tick box: "OK to share this anonymously." Box at the counter; collected Mondays.
2. **Web form** linked from the chat's end screen: one text box plus the same
   tick box. No login, no email field, no IP address stored, no device link [A — must be verified in the build].
3. **Editing rules before anything is shared:** remove names, exact dates and
   times, physical descriptions and any detail that could identify someone to a
   regular at that venue. Keep at most three details. Wait at least two weeks
   before publishing.
4. **Staff anecdotes** only in general terms ("two regulars now climb together on
   Tuesdays"), never pointing out people.
5. Never follow up with a storyteller — we have no way to, by design.

## 9. Success metrics and go/no-go

The thresholds are fixed now, before any data, so we can't move the goalposts.

| # | Metric | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Handshakes in the three pilot zones, 8 weeks | **≥ 600 total** |
| 2 | Second-conversation rate: share of chats with messages on 2+ separate days | **≥ 35%** |
| 3 | Chats becoming groups (3+ members at any point) | **≥ 8%** |
| 4 | 1:1 chats reaching reveal step 1 (first name + photo, both chose it) | **≥ 25%** |
| 5 | Venues wanting to continue paying | **≥ 2 of 3** |
| 6 | Serious safety incidents | **≤ 1 per 1,000 chats, and zero unresolved** |

**Go** = all 6 met → scale venues (see `08_go_to_market.md`).
**Iterate** = 4 or 5 met → fix the weak spots, run a 4-week follow-up in the same venues, decide again.
**Stop / pivot** = 3 or fewer met → stop the venue channel; consider events-only or a different product shape.
Metric 6 is also a hard stop at any time: an unresolved serious incident pauses that zone until it's resolved, whatever the other numbers say (T&S decision).

## 10. How each metric is measured (aggregate counters only)

The rule from the Privacy Architect: **we only ever add 1 to a daily total.**
No counter row contains a device, a person, a chat id, a message or an IP
address. Counters are stored as (date, bucket, metric, count). Venue-facing
displays show counts under 3 as "fewer than 3" [D].

1. **Handshakes.** When a mutual match succeeds, the server adds 1 to that day's
   "handshakes" total for the zone where it happened. This is the same count the
   venue staff dashboard already shows [D]. Sum zones 21–23 over 1 Mar – 25 Apr.

2. **Second-conversation rate.** The chat itself keeps two small facts for as
   long as it exists: the last day a message was sent, and a yes/no "talked on a
   second day". When a message arrives on a new calendar day and the flag is
   still no, it flips to yes and adds 1 to "second-day chats". Both facts are
   deleted with the chat. Rate = second-day chats ÷ chats created. To measure
   only pilot chats, each chat records a **start type** (venue, event or nearby
   area) — never the zone or venue — so conference chats are excluded.
   **[O] This start type is new; the Privacy Architect must sign it off, and the
   privacy page must say "we note whether a chat started at a venue, an event or
   nearby — never which one" before the pilot starts.** Fallback if it's not
   approved: measure all chats in weeks with no conference running.
   Chats created in weeks 1–7 only, each observed for at least 7 days, read on 2 May.

3. **Groups.** When a chat first reaches 3 members, a yes/no flag on the chat
   flips and adds 1 to "chats became groups" for that start type. Rate = ÷ chats created.

4. **Reveal step 1.** When the two original members have both chosen step 1,
   the chat's flag flips once and adds 1 to "reached step 1". Rate = ÷ chats
   created. We never count what was revealed, only that the step opened.

5. **Venues continuing.** Not a counter: the written yes/no from each manager at
   week 8, confirmed by a signed order form or first invoice by 21 May 2027.

6. **Safety incidents.** Each report adds 1 to "reports" for that day and start
   type. The T&S moderator labels each report's **category**: serious = threats,
   sexual harassment, attempts to find someone's location or identity,
   hate, coercion, or a suspected under-18. Resolved = action taken (device
   suspended, guidance, or no-action decision recorded) within 72 hours. Report
   records are deleted after 90 days [D]; the monthly totals (serious, resolved)
   are kept as counts. Rate = serious ÷ pilot chats × 1,000. Incidents that venue
   staff tell us about directly are logged the same way, as a count and category only.

Nothing in this list needs us to know who anyone is or to follow a person across chats.

## 11. Risks and disagreements

- **Cynic:** "600 handshakes is mostly your hosted evenings and staff." Response:
  Data reports weeks 1–2 (hosted) separately; the target pace assumes weeks 3–8
  carry at least 70%.
- **Cynic vs Data on the second-conversation rate:** the Cynic wanted 50%. Data:
  14-day chats with strangers rarely reach 50%; 35% is already a strong signal.
  Kept at 35% as set by the Manager.
- **Privacy Architect vs Data on start type:** Data wanted the zone kept on the
  chat. Privacy refused — it contradicts the promise that zone numbers live only on
  short-lived codes. **Resolved:** a three-value start type, or the fallback.
- **T&S on the bar:** the bar is the highest-risk venue. Extra on-call cover
  Thu–Sat; the zone can be paused instantly from the admin page.
- **Venue drop-out:** a backup venue per type; if one leaves after week 3,
  metric 5 becomes "≥ 2 of the venues that completed the pilot", recorded as a deviation.
